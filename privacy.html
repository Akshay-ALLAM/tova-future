Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Tova Future app ("Tova Future", "the app") handles data.

1. Data the app receives

When the owner signs in with TikTok, the app receives a login token and basic account information.

2. How the data is used

This data is used only to upload videos to that same TikTok account. It is not used for any other purpose.

3. No selling or sharing

  • No data is sold.
  • No data is shared with third parties.
  • No data is collected from other users.

4. Storage and security

Tokens are stored only in the owner's own automation tool (n8n) and are never published.

5. Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app under Settings, then Security, then Manage app permissions.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may change from time to time. The newest version is always published on this page.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: tovafuture@gmail.com
